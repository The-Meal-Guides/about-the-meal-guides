# About The Meal Guides

**The Meal Guides** is a food-focused platform built to help people discover, explore, and experience great food from around the world. We combine technology, curated information, and food delivery to make discovering your next meal easier and more accessible.

Our goal is simple: **bring the world of food closer to you**. Whether you're looking for something familiar or ready to explore a cuisine you've never tried before, The Meal Guides helps connect you with different flavors, restaurants, and culinary experiences — all without needing a passport.

From a technical perspective, our organization focuses on building and maintaining digital solutions that support food discovery, delivery, information management, and related services. Our repositories contain the projects, tools, infrastructure, and supporting technologies that help make these experiences possible.

Think of The Meal Guides as your **trusted flavor navigator**. The world is full of incredible cuisine, and we're here to help map it to your doorstep — one meal, one discovery, and one bite at a time. **No passport required. Just collect stamps on your tongue and go around the world in 80 bites.**
